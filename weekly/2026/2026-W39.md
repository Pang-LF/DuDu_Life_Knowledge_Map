## The Week's Connected Model

A credible judgment now has a seven-part spine:

1. Choose the relevant reference class before reacting to a signal.
2. Name the condition and denominator behind every probability.
3. Update by how diagnostic new evidence is under competing explanations.
4. Compare actions by probability-weighted consequences before the outcome is known.
5. Record an independent estimate before external numbers anchor the plausible range.
6. Separate source, exact claim, direct evidence and inferential bridge.
7. Audit the full measurement chain and prefer independent checks that break shared failure paths.
