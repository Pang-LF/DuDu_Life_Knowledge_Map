# FiveThirtyEight U.S. Election Forecast, 2016

- Case ID: **CASE.US_ELECTION.FORECAST.2016**
- Domain: META / Forecasting
- Primary concept: Forecast Record
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Question

Did Donald Trump's victory prove that a 28.6% forecast was wrong?

## Forecast Record

- Event: Donald Trump wins the 2016 U.S. presidential election.
- Information cutoff: FiveThirtyEight final update on 2016-11-08.
- Probability: 28.6%.
- Resolution source: U.S. National Archives Electoral College result.
- Resolution: True; Trump received 304 electoral votes, Clinton 227, and 270 formed a majority.

## Quantitative Anchors

- 28.6% is about one outcome in 3.5, or 286 in 1,000 hypothetical comparable futures.
- With outcome 1, the binary Brier score is approximately (0.286 - 1)² = 0.510.
- A false 0% claim would score 1.000; one resolved outcome still cannot establish long-run calibration.

## Outcome

The event resolved true. That settles the event and permits a single-record score, but does not by itself show whether 28.6% was too high, too low or well calibrated. That requires a larger set of pre-registered forecasts.

## Evidence Boundary

The case neither validates every FiveThirtyEight model assumption nor immunizes any nonzero probability from criticism. It demonstrates why outcome settlement, scoring and calibration are different questions.
