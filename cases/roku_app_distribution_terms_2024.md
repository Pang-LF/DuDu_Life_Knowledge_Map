# Roku App Distribution and Platform Terms, 2024-2026

- Case ID: `CASE.ROKU.APP_DISTRIBUTION_TERMS.2024`
- Domain: BUSINESS / Subscription Video and Streaming
- Primary concept: App Distribution and Platform Terms
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Question

When a user subscribes to a branded streaming app through Roku Pay, which parts of the supposedly direct customer relationship remain with the app and which move to the platform?

## Minimum Unit

One user installs a sports streaming app, selects a subscription, confirms a saved Roku payment method, receives an entitlement in the app, renews, and may later cancel or request a refund.

## Mechanism

Roku supplies device distribution, certification, account-linked payment, renewal and payout services. The publisher supplies content and the app product. Roku's public standard terms describe an 80% publisher / 20% Roku split of net amounts actually received for transactional apps, while APIs communicate transaction and entitlement state. AVOD distribution agreements can separately allocate video advertising inventory.

## Evidence Boundary

Public developer terms describe standard arrangements, not every negotiated agreement. Major services can use direct billing or bespoke terms. The published share is not platform profit, and aggregate disclosures do not identify incremental conversion, retention, migration, support savings or cohort LTV. Data access and advertising rights vary by app and contract.
