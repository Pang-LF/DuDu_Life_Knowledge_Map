# NBA National Media-Rights Agreements, 2024

- Case ID: `CASE.NBA.MEDIA_RIGHTS.2024`
- Domain: BUSINESS / Subscription Video and Streaming
- Primary concept: Sports Media Rights
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Question

Why did the NBA divide national live rights among Disney, NBCUniversal and Amazon instead of choosing one distributor?

## Package Structure

The 11-year agreements run from the 2025-26 through 2035-36 seasons. Disney receives 80 regular-season games per season and retains exclusive NBA Finals coverage; NBCU receives up to 100 regular-season games; Amazon receives 66 regular-season games plus defined tournament and postseason packages. Approximately 75 regular-season games will appear on broadcast television, up from a 15-game prior minimum.

## Mechanism

Different partners contribute broadcast reach, sports programming, streaming subscriptions, advertising and international distribution. Dividing the package reduces counterparty concentration and supports multiple audience paths, while increasing consumer discovery and subscription complexity. The NBA App is positioned as a universal access point across destinations.

## Evidence Boundary

Partner package counts are not necessarily unique-game counts and should not be blindly summed. Official announcements do not disclose all rights fees, costs, profitability, subscriber incrementality or consumer-welfare effects.
