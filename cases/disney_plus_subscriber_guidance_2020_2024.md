# Disney+ Subscriber Guidance, 2020-2024

- Case ID: **CASE.DISNEY_PLUS.SUBSCRIBER_GUIDANCE.2020_2024**
- Domain: BUSINESS / Streaming Industry
- Primary concept: Industry Integration
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Question

What did Disney's FY2024 subscriber miss prove, and what remained unresolved?

## Forecast Record

At its 2020 Investor Day, Disney reported 86.8 million Disney+ paid subscribers as of December 2 and raised FY2024 guidance to 230-260 million. The outlook included Disney+ Core, Hotstar and specified Star or bundled subscription counting rules.

## Quantitative Anchors

- FY2024 Disney+ Core: 122.7 million paid subscribers.
- FY2024 Disney+ Hotstar: 35.9 million.
- Combined: 158.6 million.
- Gap to 230 million lower bound: 71.4 million, or about 31.0%.
- Gap to 260 million upper bound: 101.4 million, or about 39.0%.
- FY2024 DTC operating income: $143 million, versus a $2.496 billion loss in FY2023.

## Outcome

The disclosed subscriber range was not achieved. The broader DTC segment became profitable on its reported annual scope, but that scope included Hulu and therefore did not directly resolve Disney+ standalone profitability.

## Evidence Boundary

The subscriber miss does not by itself prove that the entire DTC strategy destroyed value. Strategic evaluation also requires price, advertising, retention, content costs, contribution, cash flow, rights value and a credible counterfactual.
