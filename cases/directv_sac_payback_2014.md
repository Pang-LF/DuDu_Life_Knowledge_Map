# DIRECTV Subscriber Acquisition Economics, 2014

- Case ID: `CASE.DIRECTV.SAC_PAYBACK.2014`
- Domain: BUSINESS / Subscription Economics
- Primary concept: CAC, SAC and Payback Period
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Puzzle

Why does $883 SAC divided by $106.94 monthly ARPU produce a misleading 8.3-month payback estimate?

## Minimum Unit

One new DIRECTV household requires equipment, installation, commissions, marketing, and acquisition support before it can contribute monthly subscription margin, and it may churn before the upfront cost is recovered.

## Reported Economics

- 2014 expensed subscriber acquisition costs: $2.853 billion.
- Cash paid for leased equipment used in acquisitions: $0.507 billion.
- Total acquisition costs: $3.360 billion.
- Gross subscriber additions: 3.804 million.
- Reported SAC: $883 per gross addition.
- ARPU: $106.94 per month.
- Average monthly churn: 1.52%.
- Subscriber disconnections: 3.705 million; net additions: about 99,000.
- Pre-SAC Margin: $8.873 billion, or 34.1% of segment revenue.

## Payback Interpretation

Dividing SAC by ARPU treats revenue as recoverable contribution and implies 8.3 months. A clearly labeled segment-average proxy multiplies ARPU by the 34.1% Pre-SAC Margin ratio, producing about $36.47 per active subscriber-month and 24.2 months before churn adjustment. Under a purely hypothetical constant 1.52% monthly hazard and constant contribution, retained cumulative contribution reaches $883 after roughly 30 months.

Neither 24.2 nor 30 months is an official DIRECTV cohort payback. The public figures aggregate channels, plans, tenures, and mature subscribers. A real payback calculation must follow acquisition cohorts and accumulate realized contribution after retention.

## Business Mechanism

Satellite acquisition costs include hardware and physical installation, so cash leaves before customer-month contribution arrives. Higher-cost channels can still be economically superior if they acquire customers with stronger retention and margin. DIRECTV's filing also described partial commission reimbursement when subscribers canceled within a 12-month period, showing that acquisition contracts can share early-churn risk.

## Evidence Boundary

Pre-SAC Margin is a non-GAAP segment measure and not an incremental new-customer contribution margin. ARPU and churn are aggregate averages rather than acquisition-cohort metrics. The proxy is useful for magnitude and conceptual sequencing, not for investment valuation or a precise historical payback claim.
