# GlobalWafers Sherman 300mm Wafer Facility, 2022-2026

- Case ID: `CASE.GLOBALWAFERS.SHERMAN.300MM_WAFER.2022_2026`
- Domain: SYSTEM / Compute Systems and BUSINESS / Semiconductor Industry
- Primary concept: Silicon, Crystal and Qualified Wafer
- Industry application: Semiconductor Wafer Supply Economics
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Puzzle

Why would a memory manufacturer propose USD 500 million of strategic financing and a ten-year supply agreement for circular silicon substrates when silicon resources are abundant?

## Minimum Unit And System Outcome

The minimum unit is one wafer that meets an exact customer's diameter, structure, electrical, geometry, surface and defect specification. The system outcome is stable qualified wafer supply that can enter a target fab process and support predictable downstream yield, not installed puller capacity or gross silicon tonnage.

## Narrative Structure

- Before: The United States had semiconductor fabs and abundant silicon resources but no new fully integrated advanced high-volume 300mm raw silicon wafer line; Commerce estimated in 2024 that five firms held more than 80% of the global 300mm market and roughly 90% of silicon wafers came from East Asia.
- Pressure Builds: Announced U.S. logic, memory and mature-node fabs increased expected substrate demand, while a wafer supplier faced the risk of investing years before customers ramped and customers faced the risk of waiting for local qualified supply.
- Turning Point: Commerce issued final awards of up to USD 406 million for GlobalWafers projects with approximately USD 4 billion expected capital expenditure across Texas and Missouri, with disbursement tied to milestones.
- Cascade: Building equipment created physical capacity, but samples still had to pass customer process qualification before they became substitutable production supply; opening, sample shipment, qualification, ramp and full utilization remained distinct events.
- Decision: GlobalWafers integrated crystal growth through finished wafer production in Sherman; public funding shared some milestone risk; Micron later announced proposed strategic financing and a ten-year supply agreement to pair capital with long-term demand.
- Outcome: GlobalWafers reported the Sherman facility opened in May 2025. Micron's July 2026 transaction remained subject to definitive agreements, approvals and closing conditions, so announced financing and access were not yet completed supply facts.
- Aftermath: The case shows that contracts can finance process learning and qualified capacity, but only future qualified shipments, yield, utilization, delivery and disruption recovery can establish resilience and economic value.

## Integrated Mechanism

Resource abundance does not determine qualified capacity. Chemical purification controls unintended impurities; single-crystal growth controls lattice continuity, dopants and defects; wafer forming controls diameter, thickness, flatness and surface damage; polishing, cleaning and metrology control the interface delivered to the fab. Customer qualification then links the supplier's product to a specific process window. Capital and long-term demand commitments help synchronize these slow investments, but do not replace physical quality or remove demand risk.

## Numbers That Change The Interpretation

- A 300mm circle has 2.25 times the area of a 200mm circle before edge exclusions: about 70,686 versus 31,416 square millimeters.
- Commerce's final award was up to USD 406 million supporting about USD 4 billion in expected capital expenditure across Texas and Missouri, so public direct funding covered only a portion and was milestone based.
- GlobalWafers described a USD 3.5 billion Sherman project at opening, 1,200 construction jobs and 180 permanent jobs created, with up to 650 engineering, technical and operating positions intended by end-2028; these are company-reported figures and plans.
- Micron announced proposed USD 500 million financing and a ten-year supply agreement; the press release explicitly stated the transaction remained subject to definitive agreements, approvals and closing.

## Comparison And Evidence Boundary

A raw silicon resource, electronic-grade polysilicon, single-crystal ingot, polished wafer, epitaxial wafer and customer-qualified wafer are different objects. A 200mm polished wafer is not spare capacity for a 300mm epitaxial process. The 2022 full-build plan of up to 1.2 million wafers per month was not opening output. Public announcements do not disclose customer qualification completion, current product yield, utilization, contract pricing or the counterfactual cost of imported supply.
