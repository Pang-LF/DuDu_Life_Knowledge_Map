# Netflix Ad-Supported Plan, 2022-2024

- Case ID: `CASE.NETFLIX.AD_SUPPORTED_PLAN.2022_2024`
- Domain: BUSINESS / Subscription Video and Streaming
- Primary concept: Ad-Supported Streaming
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Question

Why can a lower-priced ad-supported streaming plan create more unit value than a higher-priced ad-free plan, and what evidence is needed before believing that it does?

## Product Change

- Netflix launched U.S. Basic with Ads at $6.99 per month on November 3, 2022, across a 12-country rollout.
- Initial ad load averaged four to five minutes per hour, with 15- or 30-second ads before and during content.
- Initial feature boundaries included 720p, no downloads and some unavailable titles because advertising rights were not included in every content license.
- By November 2023 Netflix had upgraded the plan to 1080p, two concurrent streams and downloads.

## Reported Scale

- Nearly 5 million global monthly active users in May 2023, six months after launch.
- 15 million global monthly active users in November 2023.
- 40 million global monthly active users in May 2024, up from 5 million a year earlier.
- More than 40% of signups in ad-supported countries came from the ads plan by May 2024.

## Mechanism

One viewer-hour creates limited ad inventory. Advertising revenue depends on impressions, fill rate and realized net CPM, while frequency caps and ad load protect viewer and advertiser value. Plan economics combine subscription and advertising revenue, then subtract content, service, ad sales, technology, measurement and partner costs. Migration, viewing and retention determine whether the tier expands LTV or only cannibalizes a higher-priced plan.

## Evidence Boundary

Monthly active users are not paid memberships, accounts, revenue or profit. Company materials promote the advertising business and cite partner measurement; they do not disclose plan-level retention, migration, net ad contribution or cohort LTV. Growth in the ad plan does not by itself identify incremental company value.
