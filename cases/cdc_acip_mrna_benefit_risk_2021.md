# CDC ACIP mRNA Vaccine Benefit-Risk Assessment, 2021

- Case ID: `CASE.CDC.ACIP.MRNA_BENEFIT_RISK.2021`
- Domain: META / Probability; exposure to Vaccine Safety Surveillance
- Primary concept: Expected Value
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Puzzle

How could a committee rationally continue a recommendation after estimating 39-47 myocarditis cases per million second doses in younger males?

## Minimum Unit

One million second mRNA vaccine doses administered to a defined age-sex group, evaluated over a 120-day horizon under contemporaneous COVID-19 incidence, assumed vaccine effectiveness and observed post-vaccination myocarditis reporting rates.

## Narrative Structure

- Before: Randomized trials and early authorization evidence supported a two-dose schedule, while very rare subgroup-specific harms were difficult to identify before tens of millions of doses.
- Pressure Builds: Postauthorization monitoring detected myocarditis reports concentrated among younger males soon after dose two.
- Turning Point: ACIP aligned population, action and horizon, then expressed both benefits and harms per one million second doses.
- Cascade: For males aged 12-29, the model estimated 11,000 cases, 560 hospitalizations, 138 ICU admissions and six deaths prevented over 120 days against 39-47 expected myocarditis cases.
- Decision: ACIP judged that benefits outweighed risks in the then-recommended populations, retained the recommendation, added explicit risk communication and continued monitoring.
- Outcome: FDA fact sheets and CDC guidance incorporated myocarditis information while surveillance and clinical follow-up continued.
- Aftermath: The analysis remained updateable as incidence, immunity, products, effectiveness and safety evidence changed; it was not a timeless individual prescription.

## Quantitative Anchors

- Approximately 296 million mRNA doses had been administered by June 11, 2021; 52 million went to ages 12-29, including 22 million second doses.
- VAERS had received 1,226 myocarditis reports; CDC's prioritized review found 323 of 484 records in people under 30 met case definitions.
- Crude reporting rate after second dose in males aged 12-29: 40.6 per million; ages 12-17: 62.8; ages 18-24: 50.5.
- Males aged 12-29 per million second doses over 120 days: 11,000 cases, 560 hospitalizations, 138 ICU admissions and six deaths prevented; 39-47 myocarditis cases expected.
- Males aged 30 and above: 15,300 cases, 4,598 hospitalizations, 1,242 ICU admissions and 700 deaths prevented; three to four myocarditis cases expected.

## Evidence Boundary

VAERS is a signal-detection system and reports do not alone establish diagnosis or causation. The benefit estimates used late-May 2021 incidence, a 95% effectiveness assumption and a 120-day horizon. Nested outcomes such as cases, hospitalizations, ICU admissions and deaths cannot be summed as independent people. Different health outcomes require explicit value judgments and cannot be treated as interchangeable case counts. The historical analysis does not determine a current individual's medical decision.
