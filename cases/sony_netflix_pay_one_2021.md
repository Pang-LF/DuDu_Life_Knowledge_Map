# Sony–Netflix Pay-One Licensing Agreement, 2021

- Case ID: `CASE.SONY_NETFLIX.PAY_ONE.2021`
- Domain: BUSINESS / Subscription Video and Streaming
- Primary concept: Content Licensing, Windowing and Exclusivity
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Question

When a Sony film appears on Netflix, what does Netflix own and what remains with Sony?

## Rights Structure

Sony's April 2021 announcement granted Netflix a multiyear exclusive U.S. first pay window for qualifying theatrically released Sony feature films beginning with the 2022 slate. The Netflix window followed theatrical and home-entertainment windows. Selected library titles and a first look at certain direct-to-streaming projects were separate elements.

## Evidence Boundary

The agreement did not transfer IP ownership, establish permanent global exclusivity or disclose financial terms. The announcement did not provide one fixed title-level start date, window length, profitability or causal retention effect.
