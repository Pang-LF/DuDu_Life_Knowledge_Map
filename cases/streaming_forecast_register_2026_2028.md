# Streaming Forecast Register, 2026-2028

- Case ID: **CASE.STREAMING.FORECAST_REGISTER.2026_2028**
- Domain: BUSINESS / Streaming Industry
- Primary concept: Industry Forecast Register
- Status: Open forecasts; course baselines, not user-validated beliefs
- Information cutoff: **2026-09-30**

## F1 — Multi-provider Re-aggregation

- Event: By 2028-09-30, at least two of Comcast, Charter, Verizon and AT&T publicly sell a single U.S. consumer offer containing at least three national third-party paid streaming services.
- Probability: **65%**
- Resolution: Official product pages and company announcements.
- Scenario signal: Supports New Cable or Utility Shelf, but does not prove incremental LTV.

## F2 — Standalone DTC Persists

- Event: By 2028-09-30, at least four of Netflix, Disney+, Max, Peacock and Paramount+ still offer standalone paid U.S. DTC subscriptions purchasable directly from the service.
- Probability: **80%**
- Resolution: Official U.S. subscription pages and parent-company filings.
- Scenario signal: Compatible with Fortress Services or New Cable; direct availability alone does not identify customer-control share.

## F3 — NBA Rights Structure Executes

- Event: At the start of the 2028-29 NBA season, Disney, NBCUniversal and Amazon each still hold and distribute national NBA game packages.
- Probability: **90%**
- Resolution: NBA schedule and official partner announcements.
- Scenario signal: Supports persistent content fragmentation and aggregation pressure, not bundle profitability.

## F4 — Diagnostic Cohort Disclosure

- Event: Before 2028-09-30, at least one of Netflix, Disney, Warner Bros. Discovery, Comcast/NBCUniversal or Paramount publicly discloses a direct-versus-partner comparison of 12-month retention or net contribution for acquisition cohorts in an official filing or investor material.
- Probability: **30%**
- Resolution: SEC filings, annual reports and official investor materials.
- Scenario signal: Improves evidence quality for distinguishing incremental aggregation value from migration; occurrence does not itself select a scenario.

## Calibration Boundary

Four forecasts are too few and occupy different probability levels, so they cannot establish calibration. Preserve all probability updates and add further independently resolvable records before grouping outcomes into probability bins.
