# Comcast StreamSaver, 2024

- Case ID: `CASE.COMCAST.STREAMSAVER.2024`
- Domain: BUSINESS / Subscription Video and Streaming
- Primary concept: Streaming Re-aggregation
- Status: Studied; Teach-back pending

## Question

How can a distributor recombine independent streaming services without recreating the traditional cable bundle?

## Product Structure

- Comcast announced StreamSaver on May 21, 2024 and launched it on May 29 for eligible Xfinity Internet or TV customers.
- The $15 monthly package, without an annual contract, combined Netflix Standard with Ads, Peacock Premium and Apple TV+.
- Comcast stated savings of more than 30%, or nearly $100 per year, versus then-current separate retail prices.
- Customers could combine StreamSaver with NOW TV, including more than 40 linear and FAST channels, for $30 monthly.
- Comcast provided one monthly bill and integrated access across its X1, Xumo Stream Box, Flex and Entertainment OS surfaces, while the partner services remained distinct apps.

## Mechanism

StreamSaver recombines acquisition, discounting, billing and entitlement, and can improve discovery through Comcast-controlled devices. Netflix, Peacock and Apple TV+ retain separate brands, apps, content rights and important product-data layers. Commercial settlement, cancellation responsibility and data access are contract-specific.

## Evidence Boundary

The public announcement does not disclose wholesale rates, revenue shares, service-level adoption, migration from existing retail plans, Xfinity broadband retention, partner contribution or cohort LTV. The stated savings assumes comparison with separate purchases and does not measure value for a household that would otherwise buy fewer than three services.
