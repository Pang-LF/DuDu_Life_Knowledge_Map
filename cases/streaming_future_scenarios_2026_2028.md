# Streaming Future Scenarios, 2026-2028

- Case ID: **CASE.STREAMING.FUTURE_SCENARIOS.2026_2028**
- Domain: BUSINESS / Streaming Industry
- Primary concept: Streaming Future Scenarios
- Status: Scenario set constructed; Teach-back pending

## Focal Question

By September 2028, will household video economics be controlled mainly by direct service brands or by broadband, device and package aggregators, and which capabilities should a mid-sized service fund now?

## Predetermined Elements

- The NBA's 11-year national agreements extend through 2035-36 and split rights across Disney, NBCUniversal and Amazon.
- Roku reported 89.8 million Streaming Households for 2024, preserving a large OS discovery and advertising layer.
- Comcast's $15 StreamSaver demonstrates that separate apps can be recombined at price, billing and entitlement layers.
- Major DTC services retain apps, content, recommendation systems and behavioral data even when distributed through partners.

## Critical Uncertainties

1. Does exclusive content continue to create repeatable acquisition and retention differentiation?
2. Do aggregators create incremental cohort conversion and retention after discounts, shares and migration, or mainly move existing direct subscribers?

## Four Worlds

- Fortress Services: strong content differentiation, weak aggregation incrementality.
- New Cable: strong content differentiation, strong aggregation incrementality.
- Utility Shelf: weak content differentiation, strong aggregation incrementality.
- Rotation Market: weak content differentiation, weak aggregation incrementality.

## Diagnostic Evidence

Direct-versus-partner cohort conversion, twelve-month retention, net contribution, migration, content-attributable churn effects, and control of billing, cancellation, data and win-back are more diagnostic than a bundle announcement or aggregate MAU.

## Evidence Boundary

The four worlds are decision tools, not forecasts that one pure world will occur. Real markets can combine them by region, segment and time, and public disclosures may not reveal the cohort economics needed to update them cleanly.
