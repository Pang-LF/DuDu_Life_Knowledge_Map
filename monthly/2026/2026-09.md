## Reusable Judgment Loop

1. Define the system outcome and follow one minimum unit.
2. State the causal intervention, comparison and missing counterfactual.
3. Establish the reference class and express uncertainty as probability.
4. Audit source, claim, evidence and shared measurement paths.
5. Expand structurally different scenarios and name diagnostic signals.
6. Update probabilities as independent evidence arrives.
7. Compare actions through consequences, value and reversibility.
8. Freeze forecasts, resolve them and inspect calibration plus resolution.
